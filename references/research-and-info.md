# Place research & upload text

## Research checklist

1. Resolve the map link. Get the official name in the local language, its English name, and the nearest
   landmark people would recognise ("behind City Hall").
2. Find the event: its exact local-language name, edition number, dates, organiser, entry fee, and the
   zones or activities. Prefer official pages (government or organiser Facebook pages, tourism
   authority sites) over aggregators.
3. Cross-check against the footage: dates said on camera, banner and board text (read them from frames),
   and the stalls you can see.
4. For every local thing that appears on screen (dishes, customs, vehicles, places), write one short
   line explaining what it is, in all three languages. These become `TAGS` in `content.py` and the
   local-knowledge list in the description.
5. Write down what you could NOT verify (opening hours, prices) and keep it out of the video and the
   description, or mark it "check the official page before going".

```
【YouTube Shorts 標題（擇一）】
<hook-style title in l1> #shorts
<event-name title in l1> #shorts
<English title> #Shorts

【說明欄】
<2–4 lines: what this is, the best moments, why go now>

🧭 在地小知識 / Local notes
・<local name> <l1 name>：<one-line explanation>
・...

📍 活動資訊 / Event info
活動：<official local-language name>（<translation>）
日期：<dates>
地點：<place> ／ <landmark>
地圖：<maps link>
入場：<fee>
※ <anything unverified: "check the official page before going">

🎵 本片 BGM 與音效為 AI 生成音源；旁白為 AI 合成的本人聲音
#<place> #<city> #<country>travel #<event> #shorts

【TikTok 說明】
<shorter version + 3–5 hashtags>
```

Keep the AI disclosure line whenever the music or voice is generated. Don't add camera or phone credits
unless the user asks for them.
